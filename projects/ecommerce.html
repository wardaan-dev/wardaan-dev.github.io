Luxe Shop E-Commerce

Overview

Luxe Shop is a full-featured online store built for a smooth path from browsing to checkout. Product filtering, a persistent cart and a streamlined, secure checkout flow work together to keep conversion friction as low as possible.

Project Highlights

  • Product catalog with filtering & search
  • Persistent shopping cart & saved items
  • Secure checkout with payment gateway integration
  • Order confirmation & account dashboard
